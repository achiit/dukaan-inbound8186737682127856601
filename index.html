Three generations, one kadhai

I am Rakesh Gupta. My father, Gopal Prasad Gupta, lit the first chulha in this lane in 1987 with one iron kadhai and a borrowed table. After school I sat on a stool next to him and stirred khoya until my arm ached. I still remember his rule: if you would not feed it to your own mother, do not sell it.

My mama, Mohan Lal, taught me to judge sugar syrup by touch, no thermometer needed. My wife Sunita checks every box before it leaves the shop. That is why a hundred-year-old habit still runs this small counter.

Today my son Aarav answers your WhatsApp messages, but the motichoor is still boiled the way my father did it. Come in, say hello, and take a piece. We would rather earn you as a neighbour than as a customer.
